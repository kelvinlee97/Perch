# Perch Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Perch is a local-first macOS application. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Data stored on your Mac

Perch stores the following information locally:

- Tasks, completion state, and reminder times.
- App preferences, including keyboard shortcuts and reminder pause state.

Task data is stored in:

```text
~/Library/Application Support/Perch
```

Perch does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data collection SDKs.

## Data retention and deletion

Tasks remain on your Mac until you delete them in Perch. To remove all task data, quit Perch and delete the Perch folder in `~/Library/Application Support`.

Deleting the Perch app does not automatically delete its locally stored task data.

## Network access

The current version of Perch does not send task data or app usage data over the internet.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces an online service or optional diagnostics, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

## Contact

A public support contact will be added before the Perch beta download becomes available.
